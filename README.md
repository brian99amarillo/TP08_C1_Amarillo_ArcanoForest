<h2>🇦🇷 Español</h2>
<h3>Descripción</h3>
<p>Eres un mago que perdió sus poderes. Para recuperarlos, debes adentrarte en un bosque encantado y encontrar la <strong>llave arcana</strong> que te los devolverá. Pero el bosque está lleno de enemigos que harán todo lo posible por detenerte.</p>
<p>Abrite paso a golpes, aprovechá lo que sueltan los enemigos al caer y llegá hasta la llave: al tocarla, el juego termina y tus poderes vuelven a ti.</p>
<p><strong>Arcano Forest</strong> es un platformer 2D de un único nivel, con combate cuerpo a cuerpo, doble salto y objetos que te ayudan a sobrevivir.</p>

<h3>Description</h3>
<p>You are a wizard who has lost his powers. To get them back, you must venture into an enchanted forest and find the <strong>arcane key</strong> that will restore them. But the forest is full of enemies that will do everything they can to stop you.</p>
<p>Fight your way through, make the most of what enemies drop when they fall, and reach the key: touching it ends the game and your powers return to you.</p>
<p><strong>Arcano Forest</strong> is a single-level 2D platformer featuring melee combat, double jump, and items that help you survive.</p>
<h3>How to play</h3>
<h4>Controls</h4>
<table><thead><tr><th>Key</th><th>Action</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>A</strong></td><td>Move left</td></tr><tr><td><strong>D</strong></td><td>Move right</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Space</strong></td><td>Jump (double jump available)</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Left click</strong></td><td>Attack</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Esc / P</strong></td><td>Pause the game</td></tr></tbody></table>
<h4>Quick rules</h4>
<ul><li>You have a <strong>health bar</strong>. Enemies and their projectiles will damage you. If it reaches zero, you lose.</li><li>If you <strong>fall into the void</strong>, you return to the starting point.</li><li>Collect the <strong>coins</strong> scattered through the forest.</li><li>When you <strong>defeat enemies</strong>, they may drop items:<ul><li>❤️ <strong>Healing:</strong> restores your health.</li><li>⚔️ <strong>Extra damage:</strong> your attacks deal more damage for a few seconds.</li><li>🛡️ <strong>Invincibility:</strong> you take no damage for a few seconds.</li></ul></li><li>Reach the <strong>arcane key</strong> and touch it to win.</li></ul>
